## Prioritized audit

### Priority 0: repair blocking or materially incomplete fields

1. Replace the current 434-character GetApp short description. The portal limit is 300 characters; the proposed version is 273.
2. Publish custom category copy where the profile currently falls back to Default. Four Capterra categories and all 11 GetApp categories lack published custom descriptions.
3. Verify the Product Details support setting before changing it. The portal shows “24/7 live representative,” while ClockShark’s public site displays weekday support hours and closed weekends. The listing should not promise broader availability than the support team provides.

### Priority 1: improve relevance and conversion

1. Put the category phrase in the first sentence when it reads naturally. This gives buyers and answer engines an immediate category-to-capability connection without keyword stuffing.
2. Replace generic product claims with field-to-office workflows: mobile clock-in, job or task selection, GPS context, scheduling, review, payroll handoff and job-cost reporting.
3. Address the buyer directly. The drafts speak to missing timecards, repeated payroll entry, uncertain crew locations, schedule changes, unclear job labor and scattered job information.
4. Use customer outcomes only where they strengthen a longer evaluation field. Short descriptions and taglines stay focused on fit, function and benefit.
5. Remove stale or unsupported language, including leadership claims, awards, customer counts, unverified trial language, security claims and broad “all-in-one” positioning.

### Priority 2: improve profile quality outside the copy

1. Review the 59 selected target industries. That scope appears broader than ClockShark’s public construction and field-service positioning and may weaken category fit.
2. Build recent review velocity separately. At review time, the portal displayed zero reviews in the last 90 days and a 0.0 recent rating. Copy cannot compensate for missing recent review evidence.
3. Keep the profile complete and current across company data, categories, integrations, screenshots and product facts. Copy is one signal within a larger marketplace profile.

## SEO and AEO approach

The drafts use the keyword map as a relevance and conversion guide, not as a claim about a marketplace ranking shortcut. Primary phrases appear early and naturally, including:

| Buyer intent | Primary phrase |
|---|---|
| Core product | construction time tracking software |
| Scheduling | employee scheduling and time tracking software |
| GPS visibility | GPS time clock app |
| Job costs | construction job costing software |
| Time clock | clock in clock out app |
| Field time | field service time tracking software |
| Timesheets | online timesheet software |
| Attendance | employee attendance software |

This treatment supports relevance, scanning and answer extraction. It does not assume that description keyword density directly controls ranking. [G2’s profile guidance](https://sell.g2.com/profiles) emphasizes profile completeness and review strength. [G2’s AEO research](https://sell.g2.com/resources/success/what-ai-learns-from-your-g2-profile) indicates that concrete features, integrations and use cases shape AI descriptions more than copy length. [Capterra’s methodology](https://www.capterra.com/resources/proprietary-data-research/) describes ratings, review recency, popularity and web presence as marketplace factors; it does not document description keyword density as a direct Shortlist factor.

## Humanization decisions

The reviewed Humanizer policy changed the copy in four material ways:

- Replaced generic benefit language with recognizable workday moments such as Monday payroll, Friday timecard checks and schedule changes sent to the field.
- Varied sentence length and openings while preserving early category phrases.
- Used direct verbs and active voice instead of modal, passive or inflated language.
- Removed semicolons, hype, vague claims and repetitive “ClockShark helps” patterns without adding or deleting product claims.

The full matrix passes the repository’s per-field Humanizer lint with zero findings. The fields remain intentionally distinct, although keyword-led openings repeat across different marketplace placements because each field must stand on its own.
