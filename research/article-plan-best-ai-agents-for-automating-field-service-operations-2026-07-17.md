## Editorial Thesis

There is no universal “best” field-service AI agent. The useful comparison is which documented offering best matches a specific operational workflow, system of record, availability requirement, and human-control model. Simpro publishes the article and must disclose its participation in the comparison.

## Qualification Method

Include an offering only when it:

1. Is designed for field service or a directly connected workflow.
2. Executes or coordinates multi-step work, rather than only generating text.
3. Uses operational data or connects to a system of record.
4. Provides a human review, escalation, or control point.
5. Has verifiable current availability or preview status.
6. Has first-party capability documentation.

Exclude generic LLMs and agent builders such as ChatGPT, Zapier, and Copilot Studio because they are not turnkey field-service agents. Exclude unsupported outcome, pricing, roadmap, and market-leadership claims.
