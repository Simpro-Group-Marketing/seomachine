## Content overview (transcribed from the brief)

This is an update and optimization of `https://www.simprogroup.com/blog/best-trade-jobs-california`.

- The article was published in 2024, so the salary figures and career-market information need a 2026 refresh. Several current SERP competitors already use newer salary and employment data.
- Search intent has shifted more strongly toward "highest-paying" trades, while the existing article mixes hands-on trades with engineering, management and coordinator roles that do not always match what searchers mean by a trade job.
- Simpro already ranks prominently for this search, so this should be a careful optimization rather than a complete reinvention. The goal is to protect existing visibility while making the answer more accurate and competitive for 2026.

Audience:

- People in California considering a skilled-trade career
- High school graduates and younger workers comparing alternatives to four-year college
- Career changers looking for trades with strong earning potential
- Existing apprentices or tradespeople considering a different specialization
- Readers comparing salary, training requirements, job demand and progression opportunities

Salary methodology note: the update should use one consistent salary methodology, preferably the newest available Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics or California Employment Development Department data. BLS released its May 2025 occupational wage data in 2026.

Tighten what counts as a trade. The current article includes HVAC design engineer, plumbing project manager, technical civil engineer and site construction coordinator alongside electricians, plumbers and technicians.

Do not rank jobs only by headline salary. Readers also want training or apprenticeship length, licensing or certification requirements, job demand, physical demands, whether overtime heavily influences earnings, and advancement and specialization opportunities.

AEO opportunity: give a short, direct answer immediately before the full list naming which 3 to 5 trades currently rank among California's highest paying. FAQs should reflect actual adjacent queries.

Cannibalization guard: Simpro also has `https://www.simprogroup.com/blog/highest-paying-trade-jobs` ("Top Paying Trade Jobs to Boost Your Career"). Do not internally link that page from this article because it targets nearly the same intent.
