## 1. Content health

Verified findings (live page read in Chrome on 2026-09-24):

- **Salary data is stale and unsourced.** Every entry shows an "Average CA Salary" with no source, no data year and no method. Figures range from $57,000 (fire systems technician) to $128,000 (general contractor).
- **The trade scope doesn't match the intent.** Eight of the 18 entries are engineering, management, coordination or compliance roles: HVAC service manager, HVAC design engineer, plumbing project manager, technical civil engineer, site construction coordinator, general contractor, safety compliance officer and solar installation manager. Three are plumbing variants and three are electrician variants.
- **Structure is organised by industry, not by pay.** H2s group jobs into electrical, HVAC, plumbing, construction and miscellaneous, while the query asks which trades pay the most.
- **There is no direct answer or artifact near the top.** The intro is a generic career-choice setup, and there's no table or ranked summary.
- **Links:** the only visible commercial link is the CTA. No internal links go to related Simpro career or licensing content, and there are no external sources.
- **FAQs:** there are three generic FAQs, one of them tied to "2024 and beyond". None answers a real adjacent query with a number.
- **Freshness:** the page contains 2024 references, and the salary basis can't be verified.
