Answer-routing guidance:

- **What's the best trade for a female?** Use the occupation-specific OOH routes to frame a criteria-based comparison; do not assert that one trade is universally best.
- **What trade makes $100,000 a year?** Use the May 2025 BLS wage table and the relevant OOH occupation page; state geography, experience, and employment context where needed.
- **What is a good trade for a 40 year old woman?** Use occupation-specific OOH routes for training, duties, and outlook; avoid prescriptive age- or gender-based claims.
- **Which trades have entry routes that do not require a four-year degree?** Use the BLS projections table's education and training fields; distinguish typical preparation from local licensing requirements.
- **Which trades in this guide have shorter training routes?** Compare BLS training categories, not unsupported completion-time promises.
- **Where can someone find local trade apprenticeships and entry-level openings?** Use the Apprenticeship.gov career-seeker route for the Job Finder action; identify other local channels as search actions rather than availability claims.
